Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the Athar Books website and Athar Books Publisher, an internal tool used only by the owner of the Athar Books Pinterest business account to create and schedule Pins that promote our own books.

Data we access

Only our own Pinterest account (our boards and Pins), through the official Pinterest API and with the account owner's permission.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data about other Pinterest users or visitors to this website. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

How access tokens are stored

Pinterest access tokens are stored securely on our private server and are used only to publish and measure our own Pins. They can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings.

Third parties

Our books are sold on Amazon; purchases are handled entirely by Amazon under its own privacy policy.
